## Numbers you can use

- Calgary today: sunrise 8:50 AM, sunset 5:51 PM, sun 21 degrees up at its highest, about 12 degrees at 4:00 PM.
- Burns and others, Nature Mental Health, 2023: 86,772 adults wore wrist light sensors, and more daytime light was associated with lower risk of depression. Say "associated with". https://www.nature.com/articles/s44220-023-00135-8.pdf
- HeartSteps, 2019: a walking suggestion raised steps over the next 30 minutes by 24 percent on average, and the effect faded. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6401341

## Do not say

- That we are the first to read the solar sensor. A watch face called SolarDay already does.
- UV or vitamin D.
- That light lowers stress.

## Answers to have ready

- **"It is charge efficiency, not lux."** True. It is the only light reading an app can get. We count time above a cut-off that still needs calibrating on a watch.
- **"Does window light count?"** Yes, and that is why "find a window" is one of the three wordings.
- **"Sleeves?"** The app notices walking outdoors with a dark sensor and asks you to uncover the watch.
- **"Battery?"** One sensor read every 5 minutes in the background, one GPS fix.
- **"Real watch?"** It builds for the device with no warnings and has only run in the simulator.
- **"How do you know it changes habits?"** We don't yet. We would measure days at goal per week over 30 days.
